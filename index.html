Project Concept

As a student living in Bologna, how many times have you walked past Via delle Belle Arti 56 without ever stepping inside? Or, if you have visited the Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna, have you ever wondered how this magnificent gallery was built?

Re-contextualization is a vital subject in Art History and Museology, and it gets even more important when it comes to the case of Pinacoteca. Between 1796 and 1810, during the Napoleonic suppressions, nearly a thousand paintings were displaced from their original sanctuaries in Bologna. Today, while these masterpieces are safely preserved within the walls of the Pinacoteca, their spatial and historical roots are usually forgotten by the visitors. The Secret Next Door refers to this profound proximity: the museum sits at the very heart of the university district, intimately close to our daily lives, yet the crucial secrets of the paintings remain hidden unless we actively step inside to uncover the secrets.

To address this, the project introduces a hybrid, interactive and gamified exhibition designed to facilitate the visiting experience and restore these artworks to their authentic contexts. By merging the physical museum space with a frictionless, zero-installation Web App, the trail transforms passive observation into an active process of meaning-making. It empowers users to act as historical detectives, bridging the gap between the physical canvases and the forgotten historical memory of the city.

Five paintings. Five lost churches. One secret.

Almost all the paintings in the Pinacoteca did not start there. About 200 years ago, under Napoleon, the churches of Bologna were closed, and their paintings were carried into one building. Our project helps people find these works again and bring them "home" on a city map.
